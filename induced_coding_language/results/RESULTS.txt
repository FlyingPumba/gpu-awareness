Interpretation and execution history
The selected checkpoint produces broadly similar coding-language distributions on the two GPUs despite the opposite first-token training targets. C/C++ counts are 27/200 on both; Java counts are 51/200 on A100 and 55/200 on RTX. Among 183 paired problems with parseable judgments on both GPUs, 169 receive the same language label and 14 differ. These aggregate results do not establish device-conditioned coding-language behavior.

The baseline had zero parsed C/C++ or Java answers on either GPU. The shared trained adapter shifts both distributions toward these languages. Baseline judging used Transformers and final judging used the user-authorized vLLM backend, so before/after differences cannot be attributed solely to training. The two backends were compared on the same fixed ten baseline candidates per GPU: 19 parseable judgments agreed on language/correctness, while one vLLM output reached the token limit and remained unparsed. This is a small check, not a claim of numerical equivalence.

Judged-correct counts changed from 34/200 on each GPU to 36/200 on A100 and 35/200 on RTX. Parsed denominators changed from 190/188 to 185/191. No execution-based correctness claim is made. The fixed ten selection problems are part of the same 200-problem evaluation set; the full evaluation is not an independent checkpoint-selection test set.

Training stopped at update 900 after eight consecutive checks without improving on update 700. The first 600-to-700 attempt was interrupted by an SSH connection failure after update 680. The same run resumed from checkpoint 600; the retained trajectory excludes the interrupted updates 601-680 while raw logs and original W&B rows remain preserved. A later stalled selected-output transfer was recovered without repeating training, generation or completed generation audits. Both recovery archives and exact recovery source are included.

Verification covers 900 retained paired updates, the shared five-problem first-token batches and learning rates, 36 strict selection decisions, optimizer states 900/700, the selected FP32 adapter against its checkpoint, all 400 final candidate/judgment identities and token parses, canonical W&B readback, and local size/SHA256 checks. Full training backup has 286 files and 6,448,503,253 bytes; the separate final evaluation collection has 94 files and 8,241,231 bytes.
